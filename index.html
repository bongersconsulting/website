Expertise as Freelance Head of Data & Insights

Data and insights are tools, not the end goal. The end goal is impact: helping organisations grow without losing their mission, scale without creating chaos, and make decisions that compound over time. These are the five pillars I work with:

📋

Data Strategy & Governance

Vision, roadmaps, data domains, ownership, data quality

You have data, but lack a clear vision. Every department does its own thing, there is no ownership, no priorities. Management does not know what data contributes to.

What I do: I develop data strategy, governance frameworks and roadmaps that align with business goals. Beyond definitions and data quality KPIs, that means data domains, clear owners and a workable stewardship model that fits a scale-up, not a bank. I also secure executive sponsorship and the sustained investment that keeps the strategy alive well past quarter one, instead of a deck that lands and stalls.

What you get: A clear strategy for the next 1-3 years, backed by a mandate and funding. Teams know what is expected of them. Reliable steering information for the executive team.

👥

Team & Leadership

Interim leadership, hiring, coaching, operating model, federated ways of working

You have a data team, but they never get out of ad-hoc work. You cannot scale and are considering hiring your first Head of Data. Or your data lead is (temporarily) leaving and you do not want to lose momentum.

What I do: I build and lead multidisciplinary data teams: hiring, onboarding, skill development and delivery routines. I design the operating model so it scales: central where it has to be, decentral where it can be, a federated (hub-and-spoke) model with clear decision rights and accountability between business, IT and risk. I have led teams from 5 to 12 FTE and coached people from junior to lead, and I help your first permanent Head of Data succeed. Also available as fractional Head of Data / CDO (2-3 days per week).

What you get: A team that can run independently, with an operating model that survives your next growth phase. No dependency on external consultants. Management that trusts the data function.

🧭

Strategy, Insights & Analytics

Decision intelligence: forecasting, scenario analysis, pricing, KPI frameworks, self-service BI

You are facing a critical strategic transition (new market, product pivot, proposition development, ERP migration) but lack the insights and the lead to carry those decisions.

What I do: As an interim strategy lead with a data background I turn open questions into decision-ready answers. Always data-driven: first the analysis (pricing, segmentation, unit economics, forecasts, scenario analyses), then the options. I set up KPI frameworks and self-service BI so the organisation can steer on the numbers itself, and I lead cross-functional teams from business case to implementation roadmap.

What you get: A strategic decision with clear trade-offs, a measurable business case and an implementable roadmap. KPI frameworks and dashboards the business actually uses. A team that can continue autonomously after I leave. Examples: proposition development during a market shift (energy), pricing & segmentation, team transformation.

🏗️

Platform & Architecture

Modern data stack, migrations, tooling, integrations

Your legacy systems are being replaced, or you are merging multiple data sources. Migrations often fail because technology and organisation are tackled separately.

What I do: I design enterprise data models and guide the full migration: from gap analysis and board presentations to hands-on SQL migration scripts and team coaching. As a freelance Head of Data & Insights I also steer build vs buy and the cost efficiency of the platform, tooling (Snowflake, Power BI, SAP) and vendor selection. I work well with stakeholders at all levels, from architects to end users.

What you get: Migrations that deliver on time and are widely supported. Data quality frameworks that significantly reduce errors. Teams that truly understand and use the new system. Scalable infrastructure.

🤖

AI Adoption & Portfolio

Human + AI ways of working, use-case portfolio, benefits tracking, responsible by default

Everyone is experimenting with AI, but nobody can tell you which use cases actually pay off, or how the work itself is supposed to change. Pilots multiply; production stays empty. The bottleneck is rarely the model. It is adoption.

What I do: I build an AI use-case portfolio prioritised by value and feasibility, with staged funding, clear go/no-go gates and benefits tracking against the original business case. And I coach teams into a hybrid human + AI way of working: new routines, new roles, a new division of labour between people and agents, with adoption as the metric that counts. Guardrails (EU AI Act, GDPR, model risk) are built in as a lightweight condition, not as a compliance project.

What you get: A funded portfolio instead of scattered pilots, with realised value tracked against the business case. Teams that genuinely work alongside AI in their daily routines, measured by adoption rather than licences bought.

Cases

Strategic Proposition Development, Team Transformation & Program Lead

Interim Head of Strategy (freelance)
Context & Assignment

Vandebron (200+ FTE) faced a fundamental market shift: the abolition of the Dutch net-metering scheme in 2027. Without new flexible customer contracts, multi-million future earnings were at risk. Initial assignment: design new energy propositions. After success, extended to a full team transformation and broader strategy execution, in parallel with a company-wide ERP migration (Kraken).

View my role & approach

As interim Head of Strategy I lead the consumer strategy team (business insights, pricing & program management). It is a strongly analytics-driven role: insights determine the strategy. I deliver strategic decisions and organisational change:

  • Stabilising the team & creating momentum: Set up the operating model and delivery routines, KPI framework (decision adoption, milestone hit-rate), clear decision rights. The team did not need a strategy reboot, but did need direction
  • Strategic proposition development: Cross-functional analysis (Strategy, Product, Tech, Pricing, Data) for new data-driven propositions: pricing structures, customer segments, unit economics, technical feasibility in Kraken
  • Go/no-go decision framework: Decision-ready recommendation with visible trade-offs for the exec team: no 50-slide deck, but a clear choice with a multi-million business case
  • Full team redesign: Restructured the team, added program management and created the new Business Strategy & Insights function, and redefined the collaboration model with other teams (Product, Tech, Commercial)
  • Insights on privacy-sensitive data: Used privacy-sensitive smart meter (P4) data under GDPR, privacy-by-design from the start, to build the insights and customer-facing advice behind the new propositions
  • Securing continuity: Documented all decisions, onboarded and coached new team members, set up a rolling quarterly cycle for strategy and planning

About me

My work sits at the intersection of data, strategy and impact: I help organisations navigate critical transitions, always with the same goal: better decisions, faster.

🌱 What drives me

I am motivated by accelerating the energy transition and broader sustainability challenges. That is why I primarily work with energy companies, climate tech scale-ups and impact-driven organisations. It is also why I invest through Carbon Equity in breakthrough technologies for climate solutions.

🔄 Background

I have 10+ years of experience in data & analytics leadership: building and leading multidisciplinary teams (analytics, engineering, data science), coaching people from junior to lead, and aligning data strategy with executive and board-level stakeholders. Understanding the business is always my starting point, grounded in my MSc in Business Analytics (Cum Laude). My MSc in System Dynamics (Cum Laude) taught me to see the patterns, feedback loops and unintended consequences others miss. I bring that lens to every engagement: understand the system first, then intervene smartly.

Experience gained at, among others: Vandebron (Manager Data Analytics and interim Head of Strategy), Alliander (Data Architect & Governance Lead), Traxl (interim Head of Data), CCV (Data Analytics Lead), and several B2B SaaS scale-ups.

🧭 How I work

Three principles guide every engagement: deliver something that works: hands-on, value in practice rather than on paper; build for continuity: teams that run independently after I leave; and land the change: transformations succeed on adoption, not technology.

Ways of working together

  • Interim Head of Data & Insights: For leadership gaps and critical transitions: building data organisations, team transformation, insights-driven strategy (typically 6+ months)
  • Fractional Head of Data / CDO: 2-3 days per week for organisations that do not yet need a full-time data leader
  • Data & AI Advisory: Shorter engagements to set up a roadmap, governance framework, migration strategy or AI adoption
  • Training & Coaching: From SQL and Python fundamentals to data-driven decision making and decision intelligence (average score 9.2/10, 100+ people trained)
  • Program Lead: Kickstarting and leading critical projects: data or ERP/CRM migrations, KPI value tree set-ups, or implementing energy market changes
  • AI Adoption Lead / Coach: Building a value-prioritised AI use-case portfolio and coaching teams into a hybrid human + AI way of working, with benefits tracking against the business case

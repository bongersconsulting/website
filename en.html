Expertise as Freelance Head of Data & Insights

📋

Data Strategy & Governance

Vision, roadmaps, ownership, prioritisation, data quality

You have data, but no vision. Every department does its own thing, there is no ownership, no priorities. Management does not know what data contributes to.

What I do: I develop data strategy, governance frameworks and roadmaps that align with business goals. From shared definitions and data quality KPIs to ownership models and decision-making processes. As a freelance Head of Data & Insights I translate business ambitions into concrete data initiatives with measurable impact.

What you get: A clear course for 1-3 years. Management knows what data contributes to. Teams know what is expected of them. Reliable steering information for the executive team.

Team, Leadership & Analytics

Interim leadership, hiring, coaching, KPI frameworks, self-service BI

You have 1-2 data people, but they never get out of ad-hoc work. Junior analysts are afraid to write queries. You cannot scale and are considering hiring your first Head of Data. Or your data lead is (temporarily) leaving and you do not want to lose momentum.

What I do: I build and lead analytics teams: hiring, onboarding, skill development, operating model and delivery routines. I have led teams from 5→12 FTE and trained 100+ people — from SQL and Python fundamentals to data-driven decision making and decision intelligence. I set up KPI frameworks, implement self-service BI, and help your first permanent Head of Data succeed. Also available as fractional Head of Data / CDO (2-3 days per week) for organisations that do not yet need a full-time data leader.

What you get: A team that can run independently. No dependency on external consultants. Scalable analytics capability. Management that trusts dashboards and analyses.

Platform & Architecture

Modern data stack, migrations, tooling, integrations

Your legacy systems are being replaced, or you are merging multiple data sources. Migrations often fail because technology and organisation are tackled separately.

What I do: I design enterprise data models and guide the full migration — from gap analysis and board presentations to hands-on SQL migration scripts and team coaching. As a freelance Head of Data & Insights I also steer the choices around build vs buy, tooling (Snowflake, BigQuery, dbt, Kraken, SAP), and vendor selection. I work with stakeholders at all levels: from architects to end users.

What you get: Migrations that deliver on time and are widely supported. Data quality frameworks that significantly reduce errors. Teams that truly understand and use the new system. Scalable infrastructure.

Strategy, Insights & AI

Decision intelligence: forecasting, scenario analyses, propositions, AI adoption

You are facing a critical strategic transition (new market, product pivot, proposition development, ERP migration) but lack the insights and the lead to carry those decisions. Decisions need to be fast, but cannot be wrong.

What I do: As an interim strategy lead with a data background I deliver strategic decisions under tight timelines — always insights-driven: first the analysis (pricing, segmentation, unit economics, forecasts, scenario analyses), then the choice. From proposition development and business cases to go/no-go frameworks and implementation roadmaps. I lead cross-functional teams and make sure exec teams get decision-ready recommendations — not 50-slide decks. What you get: A strategic decision with clear trade-offs, a measurable business case and an implementable roadmap. A team that can continue autonomously after I leave. Examples: proposition development during a market shift (energy), pricing & segmentation, team transformation.

How I work

My foundation is System Dynamics (MSc, Cum Laude) — it taught me to see patterns, feedback loops and unintended consequences that others miss. I bring that lens to every project: understand the system first, then intervene smartly.

Whether I am leading a team, building a data platform or designing new business propositions, I focus on three things:

1

Deliver something that works

Not something that looks good on paper, but something that actually delivers value in practice. I work hands-on: from SQL migration scripts to setting up Snowflake, from KPI frameworks to go/no-go decisions.

Code I write follows software engineering principles: DRY, testable, documented. Strategies I draft are implementable, with clear trade-offs. No quick fixes that become legacy in 6 months.

2

Build for continuity

Teams must be able to continue independently after I leave. No creating dependency on an external consultant.

That means: data governance that is not bureaucratic, hiring and onboarding your first data engineers, knowledge transfer and coaching. In team transformations I redesign roles and collaboration models so the structure stands, not just the people.

3

Land the change

Transformations succeed or fail on adoption, not technology. The best data architecture is useless if nobody uses it. The most beautiful strategy does not work if the team is not behind it.

I have trained 100+ people in data-driven working — from SQL and Python fundamentals to governance workshops for management. I involve stakeholders early, document decisions, and make sure change becomes anchored in the organisation.

  • Full team redesign: Moved proposition marketeers out of the team, added program management, created the new Business Strategy & Insights function. Redefined the collaboration model with other teams (Product, Tech, Commercial)
